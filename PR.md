This PR extends selective activation checkpointing's `model.ac.targets` with `<operator>@<module path glob>` entries, so a checkpoint target can name a custom op plus the module path it must appear under instead of only a module type. DeepSeek V4 then saves the activation-checkpointing fit subset (the mHC pre-pass, `o_a_proj`, `o_b_proj`, `q_a_proj`, `kv_proj`, the shared expert, and the fp8 blockwise GEMMs at those sites) instead of checkpointing everything, the same mechanism as PR #4339.

The target syntax is `<op or namespace>@<module path glob relative to the decoder block>`, for example `prime_rl::fp8_blockwise_mm@self_attn.o_b_proj`. Under `compile(checkpoint_wrapper(block))` the policy reads the fx node's `nn_module_stack`, whose paths traverse the checkpoint wrapper (`L['self'].__dict__['_modules']['_checkpoint_wrapped_module'].inner.b`), and in eager mode no such metadata exists, so eager raises rather than silently checkpointing the wrong thing. A partitioner-hook mechanism run verified what each arm saves: the fit config saves exactly the listed operator outputs, 60.8 KB per token per layer, which is 1.86 GiB per layer per 32768-token micro batch, identical across ranks, with no sparse-attention or `q_b_proj` outputs saved.

The numbers come from three campaigns. The fit-subset A/B (this branch's own campaign, full AC versus fit AC, 4 and 6 layers, the same proxy otherwise, medians of `time/step` over steps 4 to 20) isolates the policy change: fit saves -3.2% at 4 layers and -2.9% at 6 layers, and the 43-layer projection puts a step at 90.8 s versus full AC's 93.6 s (-3.0%), with throughput 2630 to 2716 tokens/s per GPU. The stack campaigns measure the arm that also carries the earlier optimization levels of this series, with the halo flag off and the tf32 backward env set: on H200 with FP8 blockwise and on B300 with MXFP8, each paired against its own main arm on the same node (the same proxy, `time/forward_backward` medians over steps 4 to 20). The B300 repeat floors were 0.04 to 0.12 percent.

The stack arm's cumulative delta is -6.97% on H200 and -6.81% on B300 at 6 layers, with the 43-layer projection at 85.9 versus 93.4 s on H200 and 46.9 versus 51.3 s on B300. Memory is the trade: the saved fit-target activations raise the peak by +4.5 GiB on H200 and +8.3 GiB on B300 at 6 layers over the full-AC main, and the measured 43-layer production estimate is about 207 to 210 GiB against about 268 GiB usable on B300, which fits, while the qb variant (saving `q_b_proj` instead) would need about +150 GiB more and does not fit, which is why the fit subset is the shipped choice. Under the B300 campaign's MXFP8 quantization the targets keep their `aten::mm@<module path>` form: a three-arm differential accounting run shows they match at CheckpointPolicy time and save exactly the fit subset, while an `aten::_scaled_mm@` variant matches nothing at policy time (the partitioner records the saved tensors at `_scaled_mm` nodes only after lowering). Trainer unit tests for the target parsing and partitioner-hook mechanism runs verifying the saved sets byte for byte across ranks are on the branch.
